=============================================================
Apply after v24.388. Install the ZIP contents at the repository root,
keeping existing files. This update changes only:
  bao-cafe/index.html

NEW: each of the six bears has 3 favorite café customers.
When the selected bear serves a favorite, a heart appears above the bear
on the New Order screen, with her special dialogue in a small bubble
beneath the scene. This stays out of the way of the order art.

Favorite customer: completed order = 2 independent reward draws;
perfect order = 4 independent reward draws.
Other customers: completed order = 1; perfect order = 2.
The separate +50 Pink Coins favorite-drink bonus is not multiplied.
Reward pools, quantities, rare-egg/card-pack support and quest duplicate
prevention are preserved from v24.387.

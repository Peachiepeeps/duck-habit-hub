Duckie Days 24.288 — Gimmick Encounters

Adds six equally weighted rare Duck Quest gimmick encounter families: Rotten Egg, Lucky Cat, Ghost Girl, Pippa, Artist Friend, and Angy Duck.

Highlights:
- 4% chance when an eligible normal enemy encounter is generated, then a 4-normal-encounter cooldown.
- 5-completed-encounter card pity per gimmick family.
- 12 new Uncommon cards (U-014 through U-025) and 6 new Rare cards (R-041 through R-046).
- Rotten Egg Incubate gamble: 50% instant friendly normal Buddy, 44% Rotten Egg battle, 3% Shiny Egg, 3% Shiny Rotten Egg battle.
- Shiny Egg takes 24 hours and guarantees a random Shiny Buddy.
- Lucky Cat coin battle/capture rewards and 10-coin gamble: 55% 10, 30% 50, 14% 100, 1% 1,000.
- Ghost Girl accepts any Food; higher-value food improves card chance.
- Pippa delivers OC-specific Love Letters; gifting the matching OC grants +50 Happiness.
- Artist Friend accepts Shop Supplies for 3 random Paints and can offer ordinary Buddy recoloring for 75 coins or matching Paint.
- Angy Duck uses Loud Quack and Got Beef?! (+50% Attack for 2 attacks, once per battle). Defeating it grants a random regular duck; Shiny Angy can also grant already-unlocked special ducks.

The current consolidated root layout is preserved. hub.css is intentionally NOT included in this update, so the user's manual Book/shelf positioning stays untouched.

Duck Habit Hub v24.108 — Quest Charms

- Quest Charms are permanent account-wide unlocks.
- Each OC has a separate 3-slot charm loadout.
- Bronze, Silver, and Gold tiers are purchasable with Pink Coins.
- Charm tiers from the same family replace one another instead of stacking.
- Shiny odds are now 1/500 with no Shimmer Charm.
- Shimmer: Bronze 1/400, Silver 1/300, Gold 1/200.
- Rose-Gold Shimmer is a permanent Duckipedia completion reward and gives 1/100 Shiny odds.
- Fortune: bonus coins + improved rare-item weighting.
- Best Friend: bonus percentage points to compatible Buddy Pon catches.
- Training: bonus EXP.
- Vitality: bonus Max HP.
- Treasure: chance for an extra rare-leaning chest item.
- Charm art uses the user's separated 96x105 layers and CSS recoloring; no generated replacement art is used.

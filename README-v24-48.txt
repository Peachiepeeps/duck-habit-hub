DUCK HABIT HUB v24.48

DAILIES
- Removed the "Daily rewards, no pressure" intro card.
- Paint Mixing now takes 1 hour, up to 2 starts per day.
- Each Paint Mixing claim grants exactly 1 Paint.
- Friendship Date now takes 1 hour, once per day.
- Choose any unlocked OC; their Duck icon appears in the chooser.
- Finishing the date grants +3 Happiness to the chosen OC.
- Shopping, Paint Mixing, and Friendship Date can count down independently.

ACHIEVEMENTS
Added Bronze / Silver / Gold progressions for:
- Gotta Catch 'Em All
- Level 100!
- Power of Friendship!
- Big Boss
- DIY Master
- Crane Game Master
- Long Term Memory (10-pair)
- Short Term Memory (6-pair)
- Memories of Ducks Past (8-pair)
- Over Achiever

Over Achiever counts achievements that are currently possible with installed content.
DIY Master's 50-different-furniture Gold tier remains visible, but will only become
"currently possible" once at least 50 distinct furniture crafts exist in the game.

Also adds a Games tab to Achievements and launches Duck Quest v08.

Duck Habit Hub v24.74 — Shiny Hunt + Endless Quest

NEW
- 8 standard enemy/boss families now have a 1/100 special Shiny family variant.
- Shiny names show an animated ✨ and any Buddy Pon catches a Shiny at 100%.
- Amethyst Mimic is special: it only reveals from the purple Mystery Chest.
- Purple Mystery Chest has a 1/50 chance to replace a non-boss encounter.
- Opening it is a 50/50 surprise: Hidden Treasure jackpot or Amethyst Mimic ✨.
- Hidden Treasure gives 300–500 Pink Coins, 180–300 EXP, Buddy Pons, and rare-leaning item rolls.
- Endless Quest added to Duck Quest.
- Endless floors use random enemy families and cycle through existing backgrounds in 5-floor zones.
- Every 5th Endless floor is a random boss.
- Endless difficulty and rewards scale every 5 floors.
- Endless checkpoint and all-time record are saved separately for each OC.
- Players can Continue from their saved floor or Start New without erasing their record.
- Players can Escape mid-floor or Leave Endless after a completed floor.

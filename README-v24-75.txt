- Seaunicorn: Seaunicorn, Orange, Dark, Pink, Blue, plus Aurora Seaunicorn ✨.

NEW BOSS FAMILIES
- Tree Squirrel: Tree, Green, Yellow, Pink, Peach, plus Sakura Tree Squirrel ✨.
- Jellybun: Jellybun, Pink, Green, Grey, Gold, plus Rainbow Jellybun ✨.

REGULAR QUEST POOLS
- Meadow Path: Bee, Slime Kitty, Acorn Mouse.
- Bush Trail: Slime Kitty, Flower, Acorn Mouse.
- Flower Field: Bee, Flower, Slime Kitty, Acorn Mouse.
- Meadow boss: Mushroom Cat or Tree Squirrel.
- Shore: Cool Seagull, Sea Turtle, Seaunicorn.
- Ocean Surface: Cool Seagull, Sea Turtle, Seaunicorn.
- Ocean Deep: Catfish, Sea Turtle, Seaunicorn.
- Ocean Floor boss: Vampire Squid or Jellybun ONLY.

ENDLESS
- All normal enemy families are fully randomized together.
- All boss families are fully randomized on every 5th floor.
- Every floor independently rolls a random existing Quest background.
- Mystery Chest / Hidden Treasure / Amethyst Mimic remains a special encounter.

BUDDY HELPERS
- The Profile's first Buddy slot is the Main Buddy and now appears beside the OC in battle.
- Main Buddies have one signature helper skill with a 3-turn cooldown.
- Shiny Main Buddies keep their ✨ sparkle in battle.
- Boss Buddies use slightly stronger helper effects.
- Battle commands are now Attack | Item / Buddy | Escape.
- Quick Heal moved inside Attack.

Duckie Days v24.161

- Double Trouble is now a true simultaneous two-enemy battle.
- Both enemies remain visible on the battlefield at the same time.
- Press Attack, choose which enemy to target, then choose the OC move.
- After the OC action, each surviving enemy takes its own turn.
- Defeating one enemy leaves the other on the field instead of instantly replacing it.
- Buddy remains the OC helper/bonus action.
- Double Trouble encounter frequency is unchanged from v24.158.

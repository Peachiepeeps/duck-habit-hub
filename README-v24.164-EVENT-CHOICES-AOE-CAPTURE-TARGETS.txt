Duckie Days v24.164

- Wishing Fountain and Lucky Picnic now offer three choices under the battle message.
- Attack flow is now Move first, then Target for single-target moves in Double Trouble.
- One move per OC can hit both Double Trouble enemies: Peep Apocalypse, Failed Spell, Star Shower, Magic, and Scold.
- Buddy Pon capture in Double Trouble now asks which enemy to try to befriend.
- Amethyst Mimic chance changed from 50% per Mystery Chest to 5% (1/20). With Mystery Chests at 1/50, that is roughly 1/1000 encounters before other encounter rolls.
- Black Cat-Fish is renamed Tuxedo Catfish.

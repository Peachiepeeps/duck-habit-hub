Duckie Days v24.390 — Home Hub and Shiny Egg (update-only)

Apply ON TOP of v24.389 or a newer deployment that includes v24.389. This ZIP does not contain the whole game.

1. Task Shop: Shiny Egg costs 150 Duck Bucks. It awards exactly one shiny-egg to the existing inventory, is repeat-purchasable, and hatches a guaranteed random Shiny Lil Guy in 24 hours. It does NOT allow choosing the Shiny family.
2. Home Hub: the six-shelf is the free default left-side furnishing, even in older saves without a shelf purchase. Placing a duck on its six ledges is available from the Duckipedia duck detail sheet. Switching the furniture in Room Makeover restores this feature.
3. Room Makeover: 'Furniture by your OC' now switches between the six-shelf (free) and an owned dresser; a furniture-only change costs no Pink Coins. Recoloring still costs 100 Pink Coins. The game keeps existing saved room colors and placements.
4. Shelf color variants were retired from Shop and Inventory listings. Old owned shelf entries stay in save data; nothing is deleted. Wall book shelf art remains where it is.
5. Room Makeover dresser uses the painted frame/drawer/knob/line layers without the original brown image showing behind the customized color.
6. Universal Clothing dresses and oversize layers now sort correctly for all five OCs: body and bottom first, dress above bottom, and outer layer above dress. Existing Miko/Io shared-hand positioning remains behind the body.

Files changed: app.js, home-makeover.js, tasks.js, fixes-v390.js, index.html, sw.js, version.json. Includes the existing Shiny Egg sprite to ensure it is available.

Checks performed: JS syntax for changed scripts, five-OC garment-order simulation, shelf/dresser save fallback and selector assertions, 150-Duck-Bucks item definition, Shiny-only hatchery pool. Full visual game/browsing QA was unavailable in this environment. Test the actual deployed game on your device after installing, especially older saved rooms and wardrobe combos.

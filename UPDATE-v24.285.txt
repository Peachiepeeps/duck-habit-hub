Duckie Days v24.285 — Book Wall Shelf

- Adds Brown, White, and Dark Book Shelf decor using the supplied artwork.
- All three shelves are free and automatically kept in Furniture inventory.
- Brown is the default shelf in every OC's home.
- Choosing White or Dark from Inventory swaps the shelf color.
- The shelf cannot be sold or removed because it permanently holds the Book.
- Moves the Duckipedia Book onto the wall shelf above the OC.
- The Book no longer occupies the dresser or Six-Shelf, so those furniture perches stay free.

Duckie Days v24.208

Duckie Dash fixes:
- Moves the OC rider into the real Duckie Dash core instead of an external patch that could not access Dash's private state.
- The OC now rides in the same moving wrapper as the selected duck cart and uses the active OC's idle sprite.
- The hurt sprite briefly appears when the cart hits an obstacle.
- Ordinary coins now maintain a hard horizontal safety gap from obstacles.
- Obstacle spawning also avoids existing ordinary coin lanes, so coins cannot drift into dangerous mushroom spacing later in the run.
- Sometimes two coins spawn vertically above an obstacle: the lower coin is jump-safe and the upper coin rewards a double-jump.
- Removes the ineffective external dash-runner-v203 loader while keeping the built-in Time / Coins / Hearts / Tiny Ducks HUD.

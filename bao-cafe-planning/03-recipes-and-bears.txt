BAO CAFE - RECIPES AND BEAR MECHANICS
=====================================

CURRENT DRINK RECIPES
1. Lemonade
- Lemon + Water + Sugar

2. Limeade
- Lime + Water + Sugar

3. Strawberry Lemonade
- Strawberry + Lemonade
- For easier first implementation, this may also be validated internally as:
  Strawberry + Lemon + Water + Sugar

4. Matcha Milk Tea
- Matcha + Milk Tea + Milk

5. Strawberry Milk Tea
- Strawberry + Milk Tea + Milk

6. Fancy Milk Tea
- Milk Tea + Cupcake + Milk

CURRENT INGREDIENT SET
- Water
- Sugar
- Lemon
- Lime
- Strawberry
- Matcha
- Milk
- Milk Tea
- Cupcake

BEAR PLAY MODIFIERS
CHEESE
- Playable in Hub mode
- Rare Bao Cafe encounter in Duck Quest
- Special rule: more points for consecutive correct orders without mistakes

PARFAIT
- Playable in Hub mode
- Rare Bao Cafe encounter in Duck Quest
- Special rule: gives more time after a certain number of perfect orders in a row

FRAPPE
- Playable in Hub mode
- Rare Bao Cafe encounter in Duck Quest
- Special rule: sometimes convinces customers to order two drinks instead of one

IMPORTANT DESIGN STATUS
- Cheese, Parfait, and Frappe are NOT buddies
- They do NOT have shiny variants
- They do NOT trigger Buddy Pon catch prompts
- They are special NPC encounters similar to Mysterious Merchant / Ghost Girl / Artist Friend
- Their trading cards should feel special; Rare rarity is recommended

SUGGESTED CARD SET
- R-050 Cheese
- R-051 Parfait
- R-052 Frappe

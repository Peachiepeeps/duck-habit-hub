BAO CAFE - DUCK QUEST ENCOUNTER FLOW
====================================

GENERAL STRUCTURE
- A Duck Quest run triggers a Bao Cafe encounter
- The bear encountered is whichever one appears in the event:
  - Cheese
  - Parfait
  - Frappe
- The player does not choose the bear during a Duck Quest encounter
- The minigame launches with that bear’s mode automatically active
- After the short challenge, the player returns to the exact saved Duck Quest run

CHEESE ENCOUNTER
- Challenge suggestion: complete 5 correct orders
- Combo/scoring rules remain active during the challenge
- Better performance can influence final reward flavor or result messaging if desired

PARFAIT ENCOUNTER
- Challenge suggestion: complete 5 correct orders before time expires
- Parfait’s time-extension mechanic remains active
- Performance focus is on consistency and time management

FRAPPE ENCOUNTER
- Challenge suggestion: complete 4 customer orders
- Some customers may request double orders
- Hardest of the three encounter modes
- Slightly richer reward weighting is reasonable if desired

RETURN FLOW
1. Encounter launches Bao Cafe minigame
2. Player clears the short challenge
3. Reward/result screen appears
4. Player returns to the preserved Duck Quest run
5. Run continues normally

NO CATCH PHASE
- No Buddy Pon prompt
- No buddy capture flow
- No shiny handling

REWARD CATEGORIES
Possible reward categories:
- Pink Coins
- themed item(s)
- possible furniture reward (if implemented)
- rare trading card for the bear encountered

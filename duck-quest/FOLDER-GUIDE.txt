RECOLORS
Put each recolor in a sibling folder beside "base".

Example:
assets/enemies/cat-slime/
  base/
  blue/
  purple/
  gold/

Each variant can reuse the SAME short frame names:
  idle-1-neutral.png
  idle-2-squish.png
  idle-3-bounce.png

That way adding recolors later is just adding a new variant folder
and one small entry in the enemy data.

SHARED ITEMS
Pink/Gold Heart Refills remain in the Hub's shared assets:
../assets/bakery/drops/
They are used by Love & Sweets, the Hub Shop, and Duck Quest,
so duplicating them inside duck-quest would make maintenance messier.

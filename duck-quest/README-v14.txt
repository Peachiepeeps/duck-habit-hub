DUCK QUEST v0.14 — OCEAN ROUTE

Meadow: independent Ranks 1–20
Ocean: independent Ranks 1–50

Ocean route:
1. Shore — Cool Seagull
2. Ocean Surface — Sea Turtle
3. Ocean Deep — Cat-Fish
4. Ocean Floor — Vampire Squid boss

Random rare chests and Mimics can replace the first three encounters.

Specials:
- Blue Cool Seagull: Fish Snack! (10% HP heal, max twice)
- Pink Sea Turtle: Harden Shell! (next 2 player hits -25%, max twice)
- Orange Cat-Fish: Zoomies! (next attack +25%, max twice)
- Every Vampire Squid: Life Drain! (75% attack damage; heals 50% of damage, max twice)

Top-tier Ocean variants are larger and grant boosted EXP/coins.
